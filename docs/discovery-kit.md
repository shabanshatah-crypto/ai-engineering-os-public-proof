# Discovery Kit

This kit is for learning whether teams have a costly, repeatable AI engineering workflow problem. It is not a sales claim and it does not collect customer data in this repository.

## Suggested first message

> Hello — I am researching how engineering teams handle reproducible AI-assisted workflows, especially API contracts, generated clients, validation, and release evidence. I am not looking for a general opinion about an idea. I would like to understand how your team does one of these tasks today, where rework or review time appears, and whether one narrowly scoped case could be measured in two weeks. Would a short conversation be useful?

## Questions

| Area | Question |
|---|---|
| Current process | How do you perform this task today? |
| Friction | Where does the most rework or review time occur? |
| Impact | How often does the problem happen, and what does it delay? |
| Safety | What would prevent an automated tool from being used in the workflow? |
| Candidate case | Is there one bounded case that could be tested in two weeks? |
| Ownership | Who owns the decision and the success measure? |
| Commitment | What time and access could be made available for a limited pilot? |

## Strong signal

A strong signal is a specific recent problem with a named owner, a measurable baseline, a bounded test case, and willingness to schedule the next step. General enthusiasm, stars, views, or requests for a broad feature list are not sufficient evidence of purchase intent.

## Recording rule

Record real conversations in a private working document, not in this repository. Use an anonymized identifier, the problem category, baseline, signal level, next action, and rejection reason. Do not store names, email addresses, customer payloads, credentials, or confidential notes here.
